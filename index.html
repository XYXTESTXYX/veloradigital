Clear answers on file delivery, device compatibility, maps integration, and policies.

How do I receive the files after purchase? ▼

Access is immediate. The second your checkout completes, you are redirected to an instant-download confirmation screen. Within two minutes, you will also receive an automated confirmation email containing permanent direct download links to your PDF files, 1-tap Google Maps layer links, and 1-click Notion template duplication access.

How do the Google Maps pins work on my phone? ▼

No extra apps or paid subscriptions are required. After purchasing Tier 2, 3, or 4, you tap a private Google Maps access link. The entire custom layer (with color-coded categories for cafes, ramen counters, viewpoints, ferry docks, and emergency stops) immediately saves into your native Google Maps app under Saved → Maps on iOS, Android, or desktop.

Can I use everything completely offline without expensive international data? ▼

Yes, 100%. Our PDFs are downloaded directly to your phone's storage (Apple Books, Files, Adobe Reader, or Kindle) and do not need internet to open or scroll. For Google Maps, our guides include a 30-second walkthrough showing you how to download the destination’s offline map area so your GPS location tracks without consuming a single megabyte of roaming data.

Do I need a paid Notion account to use the Notion Trip Hub? ▼

Not at all. The Notion Trip Hub is fully optimized for the 100% free personal tier of Notion. When you open the template link provided in your delivery email, simply click the "Duplicate" button in the top-right corner to copy the workspace directly into your own private account.

How do the free lifetime updates work? ▼

Restaurants relocate, train timetables shift, and seasonal ferry routes change. Whenever our scouts update a guide, you will receive an automatic email notification with fresh download links. Furthermore, the Google Maps pin layers synchronize live in the cloud—any newly verified spot added by our team appears in your map automatically.

Are the itineraries customizable, or do I have to follow them rigidly? ▼

They are completely modular. Each day is structured into geographic clusters (Morning, Afternoon, Sunset, Evening) so you never spend hours backtracking across town. If bad weather hits or you want a slow morning, you can swap entire daily clusters effortlessly. With the Notion Hub edition, you can drag and drop itinerary blocks into any custom order you wish.

Yes! You are welcome to share your downloaded files and invite your partner, spouse, or travel companions on the same trip to your duplicated Notion workspace. We only ask that you do not publish, resell, or distribute the files publicly online.
